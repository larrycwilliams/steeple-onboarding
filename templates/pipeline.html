Leads come from the Shopify Forms submissions on the Book a Discovery Call page. Your own test submissions are filtered out by email address, so testing the form never adds a lead. Stage is stored here, not written back to Shopify.

Past one day counts only leads still sitting on New — the promise is about the first reply, so moving a lead to Replied stops that clock even if the call is not booked yet. The “answered in” time is measured from the submission to the moment you set the stage here, not to when you actually sent the reply, so log it when you send it and the number stays honest.

Draft reply opens the discovery-call email in Mail with the name, organisation and tier paragraph already filled, and moves the lead to Replied once Mail has it. It is always a draft, never a send. Discovery call opens the twelve-question notes for that lead; the count on the button is how many were covered. Hold until is for the times waiting is the right call — the lead stays open and keeps its age, but it stops counting as a broken promise until that date passes.
